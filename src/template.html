How to read it

The most useful column is the transmission level, which says how far a virus is known to travel between people rather than how dangerous it is. A level 2 virus can be lethal; a level 4b virus can be mild. They answer different questions.

    {{level_list}}

Vectors and routes are recorded separately: a virus can have a documented arthropod vector and also documented person-to-person routes. A blank is an absence of recorded evidence, not evidence of absence.

What this data does not say

    {{gaps_list}}

The catalogue records human infection, not disease burden, severity or current circulation. A species being listed says someone documented a human infection and published it. For anything consequential, read the cited literature.
